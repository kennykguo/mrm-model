What is the controller doing over time?

Frame means (band: min to max detuning in each frame; grey: start-up, not scored); codes are the value at the frame end. Click a trace to jump there; drag to zoom, double-click to reset.

How hot is the die?

The model's point-site heat law on a 4 × 2.5 µm grid, not a 3-D field solve. Exact at the PDs; it reads low inside a ring, so each ring is filled with its own engine temperature.

Why is the ring bistable?

The ring's own light heats it and pulls its resonance red. Between the two folds one heater code holds two stable temperatures; the middle branch cannot be held open loop. Not tied to the scrubber.
