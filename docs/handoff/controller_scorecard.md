## Conditions

- g26 preset, nominal world (uncertainty nominal, variation off), seeds 0, B = 1, N = L = 8, dt = 1 us, substep policy fixed, controller edges every 10 us (100 kHz), default controller parameters.
- Knowledge: every token a baseline reads (line_grid, ring_to_line, heater_nominal, optics_nominal, dlambda_dt_nominal, monitor_nominal, heater_law, heater_r_nominal, heater_efficiency, linewidth, thermal_dynamics, bias_efficiency, model_nominal); g26 alone releases only line_grid and ring_to_line.
- Start `warm`: heaters at the quasi-static oracle lock codes (mrm.sim.runner.warm_start_codes), thermal state settled (Plant.settle, branch_high). Start `cold`: heaters at code 0, lasers on at t = 0, thermal state settled at code 0 (branch_low). Each controller then runs its own start-up.
- Steady: static scenario, 25 degC, 40 ms. Lock point d_ref = median detuning of the last 5 ms (to the order nearest the declared line); locked = |e| <= 5 pm from some time on AND a stationary operating point (drift = median d of the last 10 ms minus that of the 10 ms before, per ms, at most 0.1 pm/ms: less than the 1 pm hysteresis over the scoring window); a ring within 5 pm whose point still drifts is counted under 'drifting' and not as locked (its d_ref is a snapshot of a transient); jitter rms / max |e| / excursions over the last 10 ms; time to lock from t = 0 (includes the start-up; the A1 runs are scored from the latest time to lock + 1 ms, drifting rings included).
- A1: slide-10 aggressor (rectified sine, 113.6 - 88.5 |sin| degC at ring 0, resonance referencing), phase P/2 (t = 0 at the minimum, hot cusp at 65.35 ms), 130.7 ms. Lock error about the steady d_ref, scored from the steady time to lock + 1 ms. `a1` = the g26 default site `aggressor` (0, -30) um, 55 um from ring 0's monitor PD; `a1_pd_adjacent` = the STRESS scenario a1_slide10_aggressor_pd_adjacent at the site `aggressor_pd_adjacent` (0, 30) um, 5 um from that PD (the default site until 2026-10-10; not a realistic layout). Both sites are 30 um from ring 0 and the same distance from every ring: the calibrated power and the rings' heat are the same, only the monitor-PD paths differ.
- Excursions: exits beyond 5 pm with 1 pm hysteresis on the 10 us min/max envelope. OMA penalty: 10 log10(mean oracle relOMA / mean relOMA) over the scored window (positive = OMA lost against the engine's oracle). Lines: rings whose final line (engine line_on) is not the declared line.

## Reading notes (true for every run above)

- A steady run of 40 ms shows a controller's operating point, not where it ends: a ring whose point still drifts (column 'drifting') is not counted as locked, and its d_ref, jitter and OMA penalty describe a transient. es_sine (default parameters) is the case: it drifts toward the drop peak at 0.77-0.95 pm/ms, without a single fall off the fold, and parks at the peak (-0.6 to -1.1 pm) after 100-160 ms, where its OMA penalty is 12.9-13.0 dB on every ring (0.4 s run, runs/controllers_fix/es_long.log); es_po falls off the fold 2-4 times per ring in its first ~60 ms and then parks at the drop peak (rings 0-3 and 7, ~13.1 dB) or on the cold branch ~1 nm blue of the line (rings 4-6, ~20 dB). The drop peak is the wrong point for a modulator (04 C5). A ring that locks late is also counted as drifting while its point still settles (wdm_supervisor ring 2, locked at 26.7 ms: -0.24 pm/ms by a least-squares fit over the last 10 ms; bitstat_lock ring 5, locked at 23.1 ms; runs/controllers_fix/drift_defs.log).
- The detuning envelope includes any deliberate modulation: heater or bias dithers (dither_lockin, bias_dither, midranging, es_sine, es_po probes) show as jitter and as 5 pm excursions when their resonance swing is that large.
- The acquisition controllers without a supervisor (ramp_acquire, acquire_then_lock, bitstat_lock) lock each ring on the first line its ramp meets, not on its declared line (g26: the cold resonance sits ~2 FWHM blue of line n+3). Their lock error is about their own lock point, but their OMA penalty is against the oracle of the DECLARED line, so it is large by construction.
- The lock error is about each ring's own steady operating point, so a side-of-fringe lock and an OMA-optimal lock score alike; the OMA penalty column shows what the operating point costs.
- A1 is calibrated by resonance referencing (ring 0's resonance follows the printed 88.5 K swing at 85.86 pm/K). Near the hot cusp ring 0 then needs less than zero heater power: in the default-layout runs its heater sits at code 0 for several ms (column 'at code 0'), and the error there is set by heater authority, not by the controller (the replicas' CAL-9 finding, docs/handoff/replicas.md).
- In the stress scenario the monitor PD of ring 0 passes 600 degC near the cusp; its Arrhenius dark current reads as light on the monitor, so monitor-based locks are dragged red and then lost; a ring at full-scale heater then disturbs its neighbours. The default site (55 um from the PD) removes that path. The die temperature sensor (feedforward_pi) sees the same aggressor in both runs: it sits ~1 mm away, where the interim site law is at its long-range floor (1.5 % of Z_heater->ring) for both sites, so the aggressor alone moves its reading by ~20 K over A1 (open loop) in both.
- Until 2026-10-10 the g26 default site was (0, 30) um (the stress table, unchanged) and this file ran an `a1_pd_far` case (`ring.thermal.pd_offset_um=[0,-25]`). `a1` now has the same PD-to-site distances, but that override also put every PD 5 um from its own ring's driver site (driver_offset_um (0, -30) um, ~4.5 mW of data heat), so the old PD-far PDs ran 8-10 K warmer. The controllers that hold their lock agree with the old PD-far numbers within 0.09 pm; the ones that lose a ring diverge after the loss. docs/handoff/integrator.md section 7 compares the two.

- `feedforward_pi` (warm): defaults: PiLock + die-sensor feed-forward from nominal knowledge. Wall time: steady 2 s, a1 7 s, a1_pd_adjacent 7 s.
- `midranging` (warm): defaults: bias inner loop, heater re-centring. Wall time: steady 2 s, a1 7 s, a1_pd_adjacent 7 s.

- `kalman_lqg` (warm): defaults: start warm (lock at once), target oma_opt from the nominal model. Wall time: steady 3 s, a1 7 s, a1_pd_adjacent 7 s.
