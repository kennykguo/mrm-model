# TLC dither-shape comparison (G26)

Generated by `tools/tlc_dither_compare.py`. Faithful core re-vendored at ECE496 `ae9556b`. Errors in pm, world 0, as in `docs/tlc/results.md` (`track_*`: dither tones removed). `sine`: 250 Hz-grid plan (4-13.5 kHz, 8 distinct tones); sampled arms: the source's four out_rate/N tones (3.8-7.1 kHz) repeated every four rings (`tone_plan.sampled_plan`); `sine_sampled` isolates the tone plan from the shape.

**Read first (2026-11-26).** One world per cell (campaign: 8 worlds). On G26 the triangle does not reproduce its
ECE496 4-ring advantage: steady / drift / common-aggressor peaks are 1-5 pm worse than sine, and A1 loses lock much
harder (rings 1+ peak 5.1 nm vs 1.1 nm). `sine_sampled` is close to `sine`, so the cost is the shape, not the tone
plan. Cold start, power step and A1 fail for every arm (G26 mismatch, not dither). `track_peak_err_pm_max` is
meaningless (1e9-1e12) on the cold runs of sampled arms: the per-block tone fit is ill-conditioned when the error is
nanometres; use the raw peak there.
